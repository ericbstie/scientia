# Idea pipeline

The single ledger for ideas. Each idea starts at `proposed`; gates update the `stage` column and never delete a row. Ideas that repeat an item in [gap-analysis.md](../research/gap-analysis.md) are not listed here.

Role codes: S student, T teacher, A admin, All everyone.

| I-001 | Keep a draft of a typed answer while the student works, and restore it when they come back to the assignment. | S | gate3-feasible | Lost work is pain point 10 in the synthesis; US-9 only keeps text after a failed submit, not after a closed tab. | Fits the brand: nothing new on screen except one line shown once on restore; the text stays on this device, keyed to the person. Design in [I-001](I-001.md). | Feasible, small: a `useDraft` hook in `lib/` (local storage keyed by user and assignment, saved after typing stops, dropped on submit and after 30 days) wired into the answer box, plus one US-9 test. Clear drafts only on the Sign out action, not when a session expires, or the expired-session case it exists for still loses the text. |
