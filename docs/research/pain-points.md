# Cross-LMS pain points: students and teachers

Scope: what students and teachers complain about across learning management systems (mainly Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, with Google Classroom not researched). Sources are community forums, institutional reports and studies, and review sites. Claims are cited inline. Items without a source are marked (unverified).

## Summary

- Students miss work mostly because deadlines are hidden or inconsistent: items left off the to-do list, uneven course layouts, and near-deadline submission errors.
- Notification design is the most repeated complaint. Defaults that alert on every post, edit or annotation lead students to mute or mark all LMS mail as spam, so urgent messages are missed.
- Gradebook trust is fragile. Students report grades that are wrong, late or hidden, and faculty report override and display bugs that cost real time.
- Accessibility is weak across mainstream LMSs. Independent evaluations found screen reader and keyboard barriers, and none of 12 evaluated systems met WCAG 2.0 in one Funka series.
- Blackboard reviewers cite limited features and poor intuitiveness; Canvas reviewers cite limited features and weak advanced-feature usability. Mobile teacher gaps are sourced for Canvas only.

## Features by area

Each area lists what LMSs commonly offer, then what goes wrong. "Not offered" or "unknown" is written where the research did not establish it.

**Courses & content.** Common: course shells with modules or pages, file and link uploads, and per-course navigation menus. Canvas restricts customization on purpose, and BGSU faculty asked for more control over navigation ([BGSU focus group summary](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/provost/documents/canvas/focus-group-summary.pdf)). What goes wrong: every instructor builds the shell differently, so students relearn each course and cannot find materials ([Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/using-the-lms-effectively-to-reduce-logistical-challenges-for-students/)). A colorado.edu institutional report recorded, in a 2024 survey, 55 comments asking for standard course formats and 91 comments on inconsistent course design or use and/or requests for instructor training ([colorado.edu OUE report](https://www.colorado.edu/oue/media/127)).

**Announcements.** Common: course-wide posts that email or push to all enrolled students. What goes wrong: volume. Students mute announcement channels and miss important posts. A Sussex learning-technology blog recommends posting only what is necessary, merging posts, and removing old ones after about two weeks ([Sussex TEL blog](https://blogs.sussex.ac.uk/tel/?p=9461)).

**Assignments & submission.** Common: an upload or text entry box with a due date, availability window, and late rules. What goes wrong: when availability closes during an upload, Canvas shows a generic "Submit failed" error rather than saying the assignment is closed. The request to fix this was archived ([Canvas Ideas 16871](https://community.canvaslms.com/ideas/16871-improve-student-experience-when-using-assignment-availability-and-due-dates)). In May 2026 the University of Manchester reported Canvas submission problems on a due day, with students told to report them and not be penalised ([Manchester Student News](https://studentnews.manchester.ac.uk/2026/05/05/canvas-submission-issues/)).

**Grading & feedback.** Common: rubrics, inline or audio comments, and a grading view that moves through submissions. BGSU faculty praised SpeedGrader and audio comments ([BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/provost/documents/canvas/focus-group-summary.pdf)). What goes wrong: feedback arrives weeks late, which hurts students whose later work builds on it ([Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/using-the-lms-effectively-to-reduce-logistical-challenges-for-students/)). Each annotation in SpeedGrader can create its own student notification, with no workaround listed in Canvas's known-issues entry ([Canvas known issue](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Known-Issues/OPEN-Annotation-notifications-are-spamming-students-when-quot/ta-p/637056)).

**Gradebook.** Common: a per-student grade view with totals, weighting, and hide-grade settings. What goes wrong: at Washington State, Blackboard's 2018 redesign and override changes left regraded grades hidden. The author reports one error inflating a grade by half a letter ([WSU faculty senate post, 2019](https://facsen.wsu.edu/2019/08/20/blackboard-updates-design-is-detrimental-to-student-grades-and-record-keeping-the-time-faculty-spend-correcting-bb-is-too-long-and-too-much)). In a 2026 gradebook-feature survey of 1,337 student ratings, 83 comments concerned incorrect or delayed grade information ([colorado.edu OUE report](https://www.colorado.edu/oue/media/127)). Faculty want extra-credit flexibility and gradebook columns without assignments ([BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/provost/documents/canvas/focus-group-summary.pdf)).

**Calendar & due dates.** Common: a calendar view plus a student to-do list. What goes wrong: the Canvas to-do list shows only graded items with points, so readings, practice quizzes and ungraded surveys are missing. Students rely on the list and miss work ([Canvas Ideas 304836](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Put-everything-with-a-deadline-into-the-to-do-list/idi-p/304836)). On mobile, some assignments with next-day due dates did not appear in the to-do section for any student ([Canvas Mobile Forum](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Mobile-Forum/To-Do-List-for-Assignments-Not-Working-For-Students/td-p/544617)). Many faculty omit due dates entirely ([Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/using-the-lms-effectively-to-reduce-logistical-challenges-for-students/)).

**Discussions.** Common: threaded forums, graded posts, and some use of chat or social-style feeds. What goes wrong: students get a notification for every new post, including posts that do not concern them, and begin to ignore notifications ([Canvas Ideas 635072](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Discussions-Improve-Notifications-Only-Alert-Users-When-Their/idi-p/635072)). A 2020 South African study of 31 completers found a chat room hard to follow when the text volume was high. The Facebook-style hybrid scored 48.7 on the System Usability Scale, below the 68 benchmark, and threaded forums scored 68.3 ([SAICSIT 2020, paper 36](https://pubs.cs.uct.ac.za/1404/1/SAICSIT_2020_paper_36.pdf)). Sample was one university, so generalisation is limited.

**Messaging.** Common: an inbox or conversation log for direct messages to students. What goes wrong: BGSU faculty want better organisation for large courses and control over whether students can opt out of certain communications ([BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/provost/documents/canvas/focus-group-summary.pdf)). The Canvas Inbox is also a general notification source, so students mark it as spam ([Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)).

**Quizzes.** Common: timed or attempt-limited online quizzes, with question banks. Not researched in depth in this pass. The only source found in this pass that covers quiz-related stress was an unfetched abstract, so no sourced quiz pain points are listed. Unknown.

**Groups.** Common: group sites, group discussions and group file areas. What goes wrong: a Faculty Focus guide reports the recurring issues as uneven workload, communication breakdowns and unclear grading expectations. It recommends milestones, private group space and one shared grade per deliverable, plus peer evaluation ([Faculty Focus group work](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/online-education/online-course-design-and-preparation/five-steps-to-improving-online-group-work-assignments/)). The article does not describe specific LMS tool faults. Group formation tools: unknown.

**Attendance.** Common: attendance tools exist for Canvas and other LMSs through third-party tools and LTI apps. Not researched in depth. Not offered natively in the sources reviewed (unverified). Teacher complaints: unknown.

**Notifications.** Common: per-channel and per-course notification settings, with email, push and digests. What goes wrong: students cannot tell important alerts from routine ones. Canvas notifications fire on every instructor edit, and many students never change their settings. One student marked all Canvas messages as spam ([Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)). A course integration posted grades hourly, so one parent received hundreds of grade emails a day ([Canvas Question Forum 537663](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Question-Forum/Hundreds-of-emails-each-day-from-one-course/m-p/537663)). The workaround is per-course settings or a digest, which parents and students had to find themselves.

**Mobile.** Common: native student apps with to-do lists, grade views and submission, and a separate teacher app. What goes wrong: a Canvas to-do bug on mobile (above). Canvas's teacher app could not upload files, and the reviewer said the teacher app "still has a way to go" ([UC Denver review](https://clas.ucdenver.edu/harvey-bishop/node/20)). A Moodle study of 155 students found 17 usability problems across desktop and mobile, 2 of them only on mobile ([Hasan 2018, Moodle research](https://research.moodle.org/396)).

**Accessibility.** Common: WCAG-aligned claims, keyboard navigation and screen reader support in vendor documentation. What goes wrong: a 2014 heuristic and user study found Moodle limited screen reader users, with frequent inability to publish content without help ([Moodle screen reader study, UC3M](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/handle/10016/19760)). Funka evaluated three Norwegian LMSs and found technical errors that blocked keyboard-only users. Its Swedish series of 12 LMSs found none met WCAG 2.0 ([Funka](https://www.funka.com/en/research-and-innovation/archive-research-projects/funka-evaluates-digital-learning-management-systems/)). Note: these are older studies, and vendors may have improved since. Current status: unverified.

**Integrations.** Common: LTI tools for publishers, plagiarism checkers and grade sync. What goes wrong: a third-party tool posting grades at least hourly, even with no change, sent the parent a new email each time ([Canvas Question Forum 537663](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Question-Forum/Hundreds-of-emails-each-day-from-one-course/m-p/537663)). Canvas also reports integration issues in reviews (G2 count of 19 all-time mentions) ([G2 comparison](https://www.g2.com/compare/blackboard-vs-canvas-lms)). Google-account LTI authorization failures are documented in a Canvas thread ([Canvas community thread 15114](https://community.canvaslms.com/thread/15114)), not fetched.

**Roles & admin.** Common: admin-set defaults, role-based permissions for teachers and students, and institutional support. What goes wrong: an instructor proposed that local Canvas admins set student notification defaults, so that messages and announcements arrive immediately and other activity arrives as a digest ([Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)). Blackboard reviewers report complex setup, slow support in transitions, and paid add-ons ([G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/blackboard-vs-canvas-lms)). The University of Windsor review found its self-hosted Blackboard would lose vendor support after 2023 and had to choose between a cloud move and a new system ([UWindsor](https://www.uwindsor.ca/dailynews/node/1620132)).

## Pain points

1. **Deadlines hidden from the to-do list.** Students rely on the to-do list and skip the calendar and syllabus, so ungraded deadlines are missed. One instructor reported every student who missed work said they did not know about it ([Canvas Ideas 304836](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Put-everything-with-a-deadline-into-the-to-do-list/idi-p/304836)).
2. **Mobile to-do gaps.** Assignments with due dates were missing from the mobile to-do section for all students, with no cause confirmed ([Canvas Mobile Forum 544617](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Mobile-Forum/To-Do-List-for-Assignments-Not-Working-For-Students/td-p/544617)).
3. **Notifications for every discussion post.** Students get alerts for posts that are not theirs, and start ignoring notifications, which hurts participation ([Canvas Ideas 635072](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Discussions-Improve-Notifications-Only-Alert-Users-When-Their/idi-p/635072)).
4. **Alerts on every instructor edit.** Students get alerts on edits and cannot tell urgent messages from routine ones. Students miss real messages, and some mark all Canvas mail as spam ([Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)).
5. **Duplicate annotation notifications.** One SpeedGrader annotation session produces several student notifications for the same submission. Open, no workaround ([Canvas known issue](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Known-Issues/OPEN-Annotation-notifications-are-spamming-students-when-quot/ta-p/637056)).
6. **Third-party grade sync floods email.** A tool that posted grades hourly caused hundreds of emails a day to a parent. The real fix sat with the instructor or admin, not with the parent ([Canvas Question Forum 537663](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Question-Forum/Hundreds-of-emails-each-day-from-one-course/m-p/537663)).
7. **Announcement fatigue.** Students mute announcement channels, so important notices are missed ([Sussex TEL blog](https://blogs.sussex.ac.uk/tel/?p=9461)).
8. **Grades that are wrong, late or hidden.** One student reported hearing that "Canvas isn't accurate" from several classes, and 83 gradebook-feature comments concerned wrong or delayed grades ([colorado.edu OUE report](https://www.colorado.edu/oue/media/127)). Blackboard overrides hid regraded grades and caused errors at WSU ([WSU, 2019](https://facsen.wsu.edu/2019/08/20/blackboard-updates-design-is-detrimental-to-student-grades-and-record-keeping-the-time-faculty-spend-correcting-bb-is-too-long-and-too-much)).
9. **Inconsistent course design.** Students relearn navigation in each course, and missing dates lead to missed deadlines ([Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/using-the-lms-effectively-to-reduce-logistical-challenges-for-students/); [colorado.edu OUE report](https://www.colorado.edu/oue/media/127)).
10. **Unclear closed-assignment error.** Students see "Submit failed" when the window has closed, and read it as a technical fault ([Canvas Ideas 16871](https://community.canvaslms.com/ideas/16871-improve-student-experience-when-using-assignment-availability-and-due-dates)).
11. **Screen reader and keyboard barriers.** Moodle screen reader users could not publish content without help, and Norwegian LMSs had keyboard-only failures ([UC3M](https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/handle/10016/19760); [Funka](https://www.funka.com/en/research-and-innovation/archive-research-projects/funka-evaluates-digital-learning-management-systems/)).
12. **Hard-to-follow discussions.** Chat-style and social-style forums scored lower on usability than threaded forums in one study ([SAICSIT 2020](https://pubs.cs.uct.ac.za/1404/1/SAICSIT_2020_paper_36.pdf)).
13. **Limited features and weak intuitiveness.** Blackboard reviewers cite "Limited Features" most often, then "Lack of Intuitiveness." Canvas reviewers cite limited features and weak advanced-feature usability ([G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/blackboard-vs-canvas-lms)).
14. **Mobile teacher gaps.** Teachers could not upload files from the mobile app, which matters for phone video ([UC Denver review](https://clas.ucdenver.edu/harvey-bishop/node/20)).
15. **Change without clear benefit.** Blackboard Ultra drew mixed responses. Some faculty and students said the new version was largely the same as the old, and one feared glitches before a test ([Flat Hat News](https://flathatnews.com/?p=144224)).

## Worth copying

- **Show all deadlines in one list.** Canvas's to-do list is the students' main view, so ungraded deadlines should go there too ([Canvas Ideas 304836](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Put-everything-with-a-deadline-into-the-to-do-list/idi-p/304836)).
- **Notifications by type and course, with digests.** The proposal to send messages and announcements at once and batch the rest is a clear model ([Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)).
- **Post few announcements and expire them.** Remove old posts after about two weeks and cap the active count ([Sussex TEL blog](https://blogs.sussex.ac.uk/tel/?p=9461)).
- **Show grades to students quickly.** Canvas rollout ratings were highest for timely grades and for understanding how grades are calculated (4.21 to 4.46 on a 5-point scale) ([colorado.edu OUE report](https://www.colorado.edu/oue/media/127)).
- **Audio and inline feedback.** BGSU faculty praised SpeedGrader and audio comments ([BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/provost/documents/canvas/focus-group-summary.pdf)).
- **Simple, familiar discussion controls.** In the UCT study, search, post, reply and edit were rated most beneficial, and ease of use was the most common reason for choosing a forum ([SAICSIT 2020](https://pubs.cs.uct.ac.za/1404/1/SAICSIT_2020_paper_36.pdf)).
- **Shared course conventions.** Use the same module structure, naming and due-date format across courses ([Faculty Focus](https://www.facultyfocus.com/articles/teaching-with-technology-articles/using-the-lms-effectively-to-reduce-logistical-challenges-for-students/)).

## Worth avoiding

- **Alerts for every edit or post.** Students tune out, and urgent messages get lost ([Canvas Ideas 635072](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Discussions-Improve-Notifications-Only-Alert-Users-When-Their/idi-p/635072); [Canvas Ideas 582485](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Notifications-Don-t-have-all-Notifications-on-by-Default/m-p/582485)).
- **Gradebook overrides that hide grades.** Hidden regrades caused WSU's errors ([WSU, 2019](https://facsen.wsu.edu/2019/08/20/blackboard-updates-design-is-detrimental-to-student-grades-and-record-keeping-the-time-faculty-spend-correcting-bb-is-too-long-and-too-much)).
- **Generic error at a closed deadline.** A "Submit failed" message should say the assignment is closed ([Canvas Ideas 16871](https://community.canvaslms.com/ideas/16871-improve-student-experience-when-using-assignment-availability-and-due-dates)).
- **Hiding ungraded work.** Students who miss hidden practice work blame the system ([Canvas Ideas 304836](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Ideas/Put-everything-with-a-deadline-into-the-to-do-list/idi-p/304836)).
- **Social-style discussion UIs for coursework.** They scored lowest on usability in one study ([SAICSIT 2020](https://pubs.cs.uct.ac.za/1404/1/SAICSIT_2020_paper_36.pdf)).
- **Accessibility as a later add-on.** Funka found workarounds cost more time than building accessible interfaces at the start ([Funka](https://www.funka.com/en/research-and-innovation/archive-research-projects/funka-evaluates-digital-learning-management-systems/)).
- **Unclear reliance on third-party sync.** Grade-sync tools that post too often flood inboxes ([Canvas Question Forum 537663](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Question-Forum/Hundreds-of-emails-each-day-from-one-course/m-p/537663)).
