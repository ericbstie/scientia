# Blackboard Learn (Ultra and Original): research

Scope: the incumbent Scientia replaces. Focus: what exists, and why people want to leave it. "Original" means Blackboard Learn Original Course View; "Ultra" means Ultra Course View. Where a source covers only one, that is stated. Dates are as found in the sources.

## Summary

- Original retires on December 31, 2026, when Original courses become read-only ([UMBC](https://doit.umbc.edu/post/150637/)). Institutions are migrating now, so the Ultra experience is the one students will meet.
- Ultra is praised for a cleaner, more modern look, easier mobile use and a simpler gradebook ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html), [Northampton](https://blogs.northampton.ac.uk/learntech/2021/02/05/what-do-uon-staff-think-about-blackboard-ultra/)).
- Complaints are consistent across institutions: too many clicks, hidden or lost content, a fragile gradebook, poor course copy, slowness and crashes, and missing features ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf), [student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf)).
- Accessibility is self-reported as partial: the Reading statement says the VLE is "partially compliant" with WCAG 2.2 AA ([Reading](https://www.reading.ac.uk/digital-accessibility/digital-accessibility-section/statements/blackboard)).
- Student satisfaction is mixed. In one study, about 58% were satisfied, but the response rate was about 12%. In AUM's survey, 40% of students were satisfied and 37% were not.

## Features by area

Each area is marked "not offered", "unknown", or described with its source. "Ultra" and "Original" are noted where they differ.

- **Courses & content.** Ultra shows content on one page, with expanding modules and folders. Drag-and-drop works, including from cloud storage such as OneDrive. A new document type mixes text, images, video and files. Batch Edit changes dates and visibility for many items ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Ultra has no course menu and no edit mode; nested folders go two levels deep ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)). Original uses a folder-based structure that is harder to organize ([Madhatter](https://madhatter.aim.edu/support/solutions/articles/150000202157)).
- **Announcements.** Announcements go out by email and also appear in the Activity Stream and as pop-ups when a student next enters the course. Instructors can choose to email copies ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html), [MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)).
- **Assignments & submission.** Ultra's Instructions box shows full content without starting an attempt. Accommodations set once in the Roster apply to all assessments ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Not offered: direct submission for SafeAssign, direct portfolio submission, and bulk download of group submissions ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)). Attached files can be downloaded but not previewed in Ultra ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)).
- **Grading & feedback.** Ultra offers inline multimedia feedback, rubrics and anti-plagiarism checks. Grades stay private until posted ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Limits: rubrics are capped at 15 rows and columns, qualitative rubrics without scores are not yet offered, a rubric cannot be applied to a Test essay question, and single-question grading is not possible ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf), [UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
- **Gradebook.** The Item List is the default view, with a spreadsheet-style Grid view. An Overview tab shows "Needs Grading" and "Needs Posting" ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Automatic zeros for past-due work are on by default and can be turned off ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Grid columns cannot be sorted by header ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)). Gradebook is the highest-rated feature for students (4.33 of 5) ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf)), yet faculty rate it poorly (59% said it was not working well) ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf)).
- **Calendar & due dates.** Ultra has a Calendar icon at the top of the course ([NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). Due dates are also shown in the Activity Stream ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Calendar was among the lowest-rated features for students (3.25 of 5) ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf)). Calendar import to Google Calendar was requested by a reviewer ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/14500/Blackboard-LMS-for-Business/reviews/)).
- **Discussions.** Discussions can sit anywhere in a course and be grouped into folders. Discussion analytics and word counts are available. Missing in Ultra: moderated discussions, anonymous posting and subscriptions ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html), [MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf), [NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). Students said Word-pasted text lost its formatting ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf)).
- **Messaging.** Conversations can be enabled on Documents, Assignments and Tests ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Gaps reported by vendor issue lists: students cannot message group members, staff cannot message individual students from the roster, and students can email all instructors ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
- **Quizzes.** Knowledge Check blocks give immediate feedback in Documents but do not grade ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Not available in Ultra Tests: Ordering, Short Answer, File Response, Either/Or, Jumbled Sentence, Opinion Scale/Likert and Quiz Bowl ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf), [NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). Respondus Exam Authoring and LockDown Browser work ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html), [NDSU](https://kb.ndsu.edu/138212)).
- **Groups.** Groups attach to discussion activities; members see each other on the discussion ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Limits: students cannot see other group members in a course group, and groups have no dedicated tool or file space ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)). NIU describes group space as "functionality is limited" ([NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)).
- **Attendance.** Available in Ultra, with "functions and features identical to Original" per NIU ([NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). UND did not cover it. A vendor issue report describes an attendance view glitch between staff and student views ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
- **Notifications.** No separate notification settings were found. Notifications come through the Activity Stream and daily notification emails ([NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). Reported faults: students receive daily "marks available" notices before marks are final, and overdue notices appear on items that do not allow late work ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
- **Mobile.** Documents and the text editor are optimized for phone browsers ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Northampton reports one lecturer saying students found Ultra better on mobile ([Northampton](https://blogs.northampton.ac.uk/learntech/2021/02/05/what-do-uon-staff-think-about-blackboard-ultra/)). The iOS app has a 4.6 average from about 444K ratings, but recent reviews complain of repeated sign-ins, notification floods and freezes ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackboard-learn/id950424861)). A 2016 review calls the app unreliable and crash-prone ([Newcastle](https://blogs.ncl.ac.uk/tprocter2/?p=43)). Old reviews, shown for context only.
- **Accessibility.** Platform gaps listed in the Canterbury VPAT summary include unmarked headings, incorrect table structure, keyboard focus problems, tooltips that cannot be hovered or dismissed, and reflow problems on mobile ([Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.ac.uk/accessibility/learning-platform-suite-accessibility-statements/blackboard-ultra-learn-accessibility-statement)). Reading reports content clipping and small main panels at 200% zoom, and attributes uploaded-file problems to staff ([Reading](https://www.reading.ac.uk/digital-accessibility/digital-accessibility-section/statements/blackboard)). Ally provides an institutional dashboard and file conversion (ePub, braille, audio) ([GVSU](https://www.gvsu.edu/elearn/help/bbaccessibility)). Ally does not score Ultra attachments ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)).
- **Integrations.** Content Market includes Zoom, YuJa, VoiceThread, Simple Syllabus and publisher content. Honorlock is integrated; ProctorU Auto is not ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). SafeAssign, SCORM and VoiceThread are available ([NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)). Turnitin, Gradescope and BuddyCheck are used at least at UAS ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)). Not offered: Badgr certificates ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)).
- **Roles & admin.** Ultra's tool set is set at system level, not per course ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf)). Roster-level accommodations apply across assessments ([UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)). Roles beyond these were not found (unknown). Ed-designer staff cannot switch role types ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).

## Pain points

1. **Too many clicks and steps.** Faculty at AUM: "I have to click several boxes to get it to do what I used to do in one!" Students described too many clicks to reach rubrics, feedback and due dates. Capterra's most common complaint is a clunky interface ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf), [student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/14500/Blackboard-LMS-for-Business/reviews/)).
2. **Gradebook is weak for teachers.** At AUM, 59% of faculty said the gradebook was not working well. Faculty cited limited customization, difficulty exempting grades and automatic zeros: "The gradebook is a nightmare with almost no customization options" ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf)).
3. **Content hidden by default.** AUM faculty: "Everything is always hidden, whether I create it or copy it." Items were missed in release windows ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf)).
4. **Content goes missing.** AUM faculty: "Items go missing, the gradebook does not function, or items disappear." A vendor list includes table content that randomly disappears ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf), [UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
5. **Course copy loses data.** AUM reports incomplete copies with deleted answer keys and lost rubrics. UND advises against copying a full Original course into Ultra ([AUM survey](https://www.aum.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ultra-Survey-Report_fall2022_final.pdf), [UND](https://und.edu/academics/ttada/academic-technologies/blackboard/blackboard-ultra/ultra-features.html)).
6. **Slow, crashing and freezing.** Students in one study reported slow loading, buffering and crashes, especially when opening assignments. Capterra reviewers report lag and crashes ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/14500/Blackboard-LMS-for-Business/reviews/)).
7. **Repeated sign-ins.** A student in one study: "I hate that I have to constantly sign in with a long username, long password and duo authentication." App reviews from 2023 report the same ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackboard-learn/id950424861)).
8. **Missing features.** Blogs, Wikis, Lesson Plans and Tasks are not in Ultra. Several test question types are gone. Groups are limited. Anonymous posts and subscriptions are on a roadmap ([MCCC](https://teach.mccc.edu//pdf/Blackboard-Learn-Original-vs-Ultra-Feature-Comparison.pdf), [NIU](https://www.niu.edu/BlackBoard/ultra/feature-guide.shtml)).
9. **Inconsistent course structure.** Students said each instructor builds courses differently, which makes materials hard to find. Students also say they cannot find what to submit ([student study](https://itwiki.wpunj.edu/images/d/de/The_Perception_and_Satisfaction_of_Using_Blackboard_Ultra-Student_Perspectives.pdf)).
10. **Accidental actions with no undo.** Vendor requests include undo for deletions, unposting accidentally posted marks, and blocking accidental release of Turnitin or Gradescope results ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
11. **Dated design and steep learning curve.** Reviewers call Blackboard "counter-intuitive" with a "sharp learning curve" and "feels old-school." Northampton staff said the learning curve took time ([G2](https://g2.com/products/blackboard-learn/reviews/blackboard-learn-review-1184707), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/14500/Blackboard-LMS-for-Business/reviews/), [Northampton](https://blogs.northampton.ac.uk/learntech/2021/02/05/what-do-uon-staff-think-about-blackboard-ultra/), [Madhatter](https://madhatter.aim.edu/support/solutions/articles/150000202157)).
12. **Original-specific faults.** Syllabus cannot be updated after first upload, images cannot be pasted in some cases, test creation crashes the browser, and grader view mangles some documents ([UAS](https://uas.alaska.edu/celt/knowledge-base/bb-known-issues.html)).
13. **Accessibility.** Partial WCAG conformance and keyboard, zoom and labelling gaps (see Features, Accessibility) ([Reading](https://www.reading.ac.uk/digital-accessibility/digital-accessibility-section/statements/blackboard), [Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.ac.uk/accessibility/learning-platform-suite-accessibility-statements/blackboard-ultra-learn-accessibility-statement)).
14. **Cost and fit.** Reviewers call it "a bit pricey"; one says it is "good for a small school or business, but not larger ones" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/14500/Blackboard-LMS-for-Business/reviews/)).

Reddit: no Reddit threads were retrievable through search. Pain points rely on reviews, institutional surveys, vendor issue lists and study reports.

## Worth copying

- One page per course with expanding modules and drag-and-drop (UND).
- Gradebook default view with "Needs Grading" and "Needs Posting" (UND).
- Accommodations set once and applied everywhere (UND).
- Announcements that show in the activity stream and as a pop-up on next visit (UND).
- Batch Edit for dates and visibility (UND).
- Conversations on content and assignments, so students can ask questions in context (UND).
- Students rate the gradebook and assignments highest, so keep those simple and visible (student study).
- Ultra's cleaner look and modern interface (Northampton, Madhatter).

## Worth avoiding

- Hidden-by-default content and copied items (AUM).
- Automatic zeros on by default (UND, AUM).
- Nested menus and extra clicks to reach common tasks (AUM, Capterra).
- Copy tools that drop answer keys, rubrics or tools (AUM, UAS).
- Repeated logins and heavy page loads (student study, App Store).
- Non-undoable deletes and accidental result releases (UAS).
- Bulk-upload friction, such as large-file limits (Capterra).
- Partial accessibility, including unmarked headings and keyboard traps (Canterbury, Reading).
- Inconsistent per-instructor course structures with no template (student study).
